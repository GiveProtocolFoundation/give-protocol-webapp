## Item 3 — Art. 9 handling note (incidental special-category) — ✅ COUNSEL-ACCEPTED (Option B, layered)

**Counsel disposition:** GIV-652 memo §4, BOARD-ACCEPTED (confirmation `c7fcdc8d`, 2026-07-13). Canonical memo = GIV-652#document-counsel-memo (also `plans/GIV-652-art9-counsel-memo.md`). The layered position below **replaces** the prior "purpose-based non-Art.9" controller position as the primary framing.

- **Risk:** A donation record may _indirectly_ reveal religious / political / philosophical affiliation (Recital 51) via the beneficiary charity the donor selects.
- **Primary analysis (content-based test):** Art. 9 **is engaged** insofar as a donation record is _liable indirectly to reveal_ affiliation. This follows the CJEU content-based "liable to reveal" standard — **C-184/20** (_OT v Vyriausioji tarnybinės etikos komisija_, 1 Aug 2022) and **C-252/21** (_Meta Platforms v Bundeskartellamt_, 4 July 2023) — which controls over the older purpose/inference-based reading. Applies in both EU and UK (DataRep EU + UK reps, GIV-543).
- **Operative Art. 9(2) condition:** **Art. 9(2)(a) explicit consent**, captured at donation confirmation. The donation is a deliberate, affirmative, specific act by the data subject directed at the named charity; explicitness satisfied by an express statement at confirmation (EDPB Guidelines 05/2020 §§93–94).
- **Art. 7(4) bundling:** Acceptable — processing the donor→charity record is _objectively necessary to execute the transaction the data subject requests_ (EDPB 05/2020 §§26–31); not consent tied to unrelated processing.
- **Withdrawal:** Maps to the existing erasure/objection pathway, subject to the **Art. 17(3)(b)** tax/accounting retention carve-out for receipts/accounting records.
- **Scope:** Prospective-only from consent-capture deployment; historical records remain covered by the layered position + safeguards below (record-proportionality rationale).
- **Retained safeguards (Option A substance, VERBATIM — now recorded as safeguards/mitigations, not as the gateway test):** purpose limited to transaction facilitation; **no** segmentation, targeting, or analytics keyed on inferred affiliation; access-controlled records; `privacy-export` decrypts PII only for the requesting subject (`tryDecryptPII` via Supabase vault); no profiling pipeline consumes charity-choice as a sensitivity signal.
- **Fallback defence (independent alternative position):** Should a forum apply the older inference-purpose approach (e.g., legacy UK analysis), the Option A rationale (transaction-facilitation purpose; no inference/profiling of affiliation) stands as an independent alternative position. Belt-and-braces; recorded at no cost in the RoPA.
- **Engineering dependency (does NOT gate this bump):** Consent-capture implementation is tracked as a separate CTO-owned sibling child (memo §5/§6; `plans/GIV-654-consent-capture-scope.md`). The RoPA records the condition and its implementation plan; the v1.6 bump is not gated on shipped engineering.
